GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

A few helpful things to know about learning here.

Are the lessons really free?+

Yes. The 10 beginner lessons are free to use. More advanced and premium content is marked as coming soon.

Do I need to know the Thai alphabet?+

No. Lessons show Thai script, English meaning, and beginner-friendly romanization. Learning the script later can help with reading and pronunciation.

Can I learn to speak Thai online?+

You can practice phrases and quizzes here. Private online lessons may be requested through the booking page once real contact details have been configured.

Will my lesson progress be saved?+

Completed lessons are saved in this browser on this device using LocalStorage. Progress is not synced or shared.
